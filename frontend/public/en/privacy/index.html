Privacy Policy

Junlab (“we”, “us”) operates Trazo (https://trazo.runvia.app, the “Service”), a running and cycling route planner. This policy explains what personal data we process, why, how long we keep it, who we share it with, and how you can exercise your rights. We comply with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and, where applicable, the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Effective date: November 9, 2026 (previous version: October 20, 2026). The Korean version is available at /privacy/; if the two versions differ, the Korean version prevails to the extent permitted by law.

1. Data we process and why

Planning routes, routing, exporting files and browsing shared or community courses work without an account. If you do not sign in, we only process the automatically generated data listed below.

When you press “my location”, your browser provides your current position only to move the map; it is not sent to or stored on our servers. To show area names for courses (for example, “Yeouido-dong, Yeongdeungpo-gu, Seoul”), we send the coordinates of a few points along a course to the Kakao Local API. No information that identifies you is sent with them.

Legal bases (GDPR). Performance of the service you request (account, courses, connections you set up), our legitimate interests (security, cookie-free analytics, operating the Service), and your consent (Google Analytics, AI assistant and Garmin connections, which you can withdraw at any time).

2. Retention

  • Account data and content you create: until you ask us to delete it. Courses you delete in the app are hidden immediately; to erase them from our servers completely, contact us.
  • AI assistant connection data: until you disconnect in the Connection Center or ask us to delete your data. Access tokens expire after 1 hour and refresh tokens after 60 days of non-use.
  • Garmin Connect integration data: until you disconnect or ask us to delete it. When you disconnect, we delete your Garmin tokens immediately; imported activities are deleted when you choose to delete them or ask us to.
  • Server access logs: deleted once no longer needed for operations and security, according to our log rotation.
  • runvia analytics raw records: 720 days.
  • Google Analytics data: 2 months.

Where another law requires us to keep data for longer, we keep it for that period only.

3. Deletion

When the retention period ends or the purpose is fulfilled, we delete personal data without delay. Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it with third parties, except:

  • AI assistants you connect. If you connect Trazo to an AI service such as ChatGPT (OpenAI) or Claude (Anthropic) and approve access, that service can read, at your request, your courses (including private and link-shared ones), folder names and public course information. We never provide your email address or your like and comment history. Data sent to the AI service is processed under that service’s privacy policy. You can disconnect at any time in the Connection Center.
  • Garmin Connect, if you connect it. We send the courses you choose to your Garmin Connect account.
  • Legal requirements. When required by law, for example at the request of investigative authorities.

Courses you set to “link sharing” or “public”, together with your nickname, and comments on public courses, are visible to others within the scope you choose.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data, object to processing, or withdraw consent at any time. You can edit or delete your nickname and courses directly in the app; to delete your account and all data, email us at the address below. We respond within 10 days of receiving your request (and in any case within the period required by applicable law). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

The Service is not directed to children under 14. If we learn that we hold personal data of a child under 14, we delete it without delay.

8. Analytics and how to opt out

We use the two analytics tools below to understand usage and improve the Service. We do not use them for advertising or personalized ads.

runvia analytics (no cookies)

A self-hosted analytics tool (runvia.app). It records pages visited, referrer, menus and buttons used, time on page, country, device and browser, screen size, JavaScript errors and the preceding interactions. It sets no cookies and distinguishes visitors only per day using a hash of the date, IP address and browser.

Google Analytics 4 (uses cookies)

Google Analytics stores cookies starting with _ga and collects pages visited, visit time and duration, referrer, device and browser information, and approximate location derived from your IP address. We remove shared-course link tokens and course titles before sending data, and we do not use Google signals or advertising features. Visitors whose browser time zone is in Europe see a consent banner, and Google Analytics does not load until they accept.

Google Analytics setting — Checking this browser’s setting…

If you decline, Google Analytics is not loaded in this browser and existing GA cookies are removed. A Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal is also treated as declining. You can also block cookies in your browser or install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

9. Security

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Signed-in requests are processed only after we verify the signature and expiry of the Google sign-in token. AI assistant connection tokens are stored only as hashes and grant read-only access. The database cannot be reached directly from outside, and access to personal data is limited to the operator.
