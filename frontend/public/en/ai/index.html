ChatGPT · Claude · MCP

Ask your AI for a running route — and get an answer based on real route data

Trazo is a running and cycling route planner for South Korea. Connect it to ChatGPT or Claude and your assistant can read your saved routes and public community routes, including distance, elevation gain, gradient breakdown, climbs, per-kilometre elevation and start/end locations.

Connect now

What you can ask

Recommend a flat 10 km running route along the Han RiverThe AI looks up the place, searches routes passing nearby, and filters by distance and climbing.
Is my Namsan route good for hill repeats?It reads the climbs and gradient distribution of your route and explains which segment to use.
Find a 40 km cycling loop starting near YeouidoIt compares public cycling routes by distance, elevation and route type (loop, out-and-back, point-to-point).

How to connect

https://trazo.runvia.app/mcp

  1. Claude: Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector, then paste the URL.
  2. ChatGPT: Settings → Apps → Advanced settings → enable Developer mode → Create, paste the URL and choose OAuth.
  3. Sign in to Trazo with Google in the window that opens and click Allow.

The connection is read-only, and you can disconnect at any time from the Trazo connection center. The Trazo app is currently in Korean.
